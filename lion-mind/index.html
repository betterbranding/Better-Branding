Meet your guide

Hi, I'm Andrea. I'll meet you where you are.

For more than 16 years, I've walked alongside children, adults and families through some of life's hardest seasons. My roots are in medical and forensic social work, so I'm comfortable with the heavy stuff: complex health diagnoses, aging, domestic violence, grief, and the moments when life changes overnight.

Therapy with me is collaborative, practical and, yes, sometimes funny. We'll build on your strengths, untangle what's keeping you stuck, and develop tools that actually fit your life. Outside the office you'll find me running, working a puzzle, or connecting with friends and my community.
